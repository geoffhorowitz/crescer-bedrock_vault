## Key Highlights & Discussion Points

### New Southern California Mission Data Evaluation Strategy
- Received ~300 side-scan XTF files covering a 2 km × 2 km area off Southern California with >1,000 picked contacts from Bridget (Bedrock) (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- The dataset lacks ground truth annotations. Geoff uploaded the files to Wall-E for qualitative inference using the V4 model to evaluate current capabilities before committing resources to manual labeling (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Milestone 2 Closure & Presentation Strategy
- Established a 3-tier proof structure for the Milestone 2 final presentation (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md):
  1. **Baseline SOW 1 Model**: Demonstrates poor performance on updated datasets.
  2. **Fine-Tuned/Retrained SOW 1 Model**: Shows slight improvement but insufficient gain.
  3. **V4 Model**: Demonstrates substantial performance improvement driven by loss function (Tversky/focal dice) and architectural updates.
- Sachin preparing slides detailing false positive and false negative analysis (showing false positives visually resemble UXO targets while false negatives remain low) (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Geographic K-Fold Cross-Validation Implementation
- Addressed the small validation set constraint (~11 unique UXO objects across datasets) (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- Implemented spatial partitioning across 5 folds using geographic locations and unique object IDs to ensure zero data leakage between training and validation folds (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- Experimental plan to evaluate V3, V4, and V3 + Tversky loss across the 5 K-folds (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Synthetic Data Generation & Augmentation Strategy
- **Augmentation Lesson**: Dropped standard automated augmentations in V4 because they reduced recall and increased false positives; Milestone 2 closure will restrict synthetic additions to verified cut-and-paste object placement (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Real Backgrounds + Synthetic Foregrounds**: Reserve all real Area-of-Interest (AOI) data for validation; train on real backgrounds combined with synthetic foreground objects generated via closed-source prompt engineering (Ratul) and procedural agents (Sachin) (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Flow Matching Background Model**: Pratyaksh trained background generator with flow matching loss. Classifier guidance weight of 1 preserves quality (weight 5 distorts images); 25 diffusion steps outperform 10 steps on low-representation datasets like ANTX (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **ResNet-50 Embedding Noise Filtering**: Implemented DBSCAN clustering on ResNet-50 embeddings to isolate distorted/noise synthetic images. Next step is generating 100,000 synthetic examples, clustering them, and sampling representatively across clusters (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_03 12_28 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
