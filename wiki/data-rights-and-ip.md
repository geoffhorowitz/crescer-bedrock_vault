# Data Rights And Ip

**Summary**: The SOWs distinguish Bedrock-owned data and deliverables from Crescer-owned pre-existing code and generic training assets, with additional restrictions around magnetometer fusion.

**Last updated**: 2026-07-15

---

## Initial SOW

The initial SOW states that Bedrock data, labels, and related metadata remain Bedrock property, with Crescer AI receiving a limited right to use them only for the SOW services (source: Internal Bedrock x CrescerAI Initial SOW.md).

The initial SOW says Bedrock will own trained model weights, configuration files, and Bedrock-specific inference-time preprocessing and post-processing code after final payment for a production model; Crescer AI retains pre-existing code, libraries, and generic training pipelines (source: Internal Bedrock x CrescerAI Initial SOW.md).

Crescer AI may not use Bedrock data or deliverables for other clients or commercial purposes without Bedrock's prior written consent (source: Internal Bedrock x CrescerAI Initial SOW.md).

## SOW 2 Additions

SOW 2 states that mag fusion methodology, forward modeling approaches, and synthetic mag data pipelines developed during the engagement are Deliverables IPR owned by Bedrock (source: Bedrock SOW 2.md).

SOW 2 restricts Crescer AI from developing, offering, or delivering magnetometer data fusion services or capabilities for subsea survey applications to third parties during the SOW term and for 12 months afterward without Bedrock's prior written consent (source: Bedrock SOW 2.md).

SOW 2 states that Bedrock owns all SSS and mag datasets it provides and all model weights, configuration files, and inference-time code produced as Deliverables (source: Bedrock SOW 2.md).
