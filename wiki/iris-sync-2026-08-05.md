# Iris Sync - August 5, 2026

**Summary**: The team refined data processing by adopting a 100m × 100m geographic grid for K-fold spatial partitioning while maintaining 128×128 raster patches for inference, confirmed V4 as the baseline model standard, prioritized object-level precision/recall metrics, shelved GANs in favor of GPT image-to-image prompts and image decomposition techniques, and defined minimum pixel filtering rules for inference delivery.

**Last updated**: 2026-08-05

---

## Key Highlights & Discussion Points

### K-Fold Geographic Grid Partitioning vs. Patch Inference
- **100m Geographic Grid Split**: Shifted K-fold cross-validation dataset partitioning from 128×128 pixel grids to a 100m × 100m geographic map grid to resolve boundary cutoffs and eliminate loss of background areas when separating port and starboard channels (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Scope Clarification**: The 100m geographic grid applies specifically to spatial partitioning for K-fold cross-validation boundaries. General model inference continues to utilize 128×128 raster patches (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Model Standards & Metric Evaluation Policy
- **V4 Model Standard**: V4 confirmed as the gold standard baseline for ongoing development, superseding V3 due to V3's high false-positive rate (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Object-Level Metric Priority**: Mandated evaluating performance using object-level precision and recall metrics rather than pixel-level IoU metrics, which can obscure true target detection efficacy (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **K-Fold Relevance for Unseen Data**: Concluded K-fold structure is omitted when testing brand-new unseen client datasets since models are not being retrained on that data; direct visualization and metric evaluation on the new dataset is prioritized (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Inference Output Configuration & AOI Filters**: Specified adding size filters to exclude small non-target items (<2,000–3,000 pixels for AOI Big) and establishing output configurations so client deliverables can isolate UXO and combined small black objects while suppressing unwanted classifications (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Synthetic Data Generation & Decomposition Techniques
- **Novel Object Generation via GPT Prompts**: Ratul implemented GPT / nano banana image-to-image master prompts to produce novel UXO object crops, preserving blob-like morphological structure while generating synthetic variations (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **GAN Discontinuation**: Formally shelved GAN-based synthetic generation due to high data volume requirements and training instability; LoRA fine-tuning was also confirmed unsuccessful for target generation (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Image Decomposition Prototypes**: Hemanth introduced prototypes decomposing sonar images into structure, speckle, and base layer components (using EMD-like algorithms) to normalize and blend synthetic crops seamlessly into background textures (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Texture & Synthesis Methods**: Explored VGG statistics for sand ripple generation and style transfer remixing for content/style blending; confirmed Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) is ineffective for sonar signal synthesis (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Background Selection**: Recommended VW and DRN datasets as optimal backgrounds for synthetic compositing over ENTX, ICS, or POE (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_05 12_24 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
