## V4 Model Loss Optimization & False Positive Reduction

- **Focal Dice Loss**: Implementation of Focal Dice loss in the V4 model resulted in a major reduction in false positives.
  - False positive counts: UXO FP dropped to 12, AOI big to 83, black patch to 179 (total ~200, down from the 1,100–1,500 range in earlier iterations).
  - Precision improved significantly overall.
  - Recall saw an 8% drop for `AOI big`, but improved across other classes.
- **Loss Function Stability**: Pratyaksh noted that Focal Dice loss can be unstable on its own and is typically paired with Binary Cross Entropy (BCE).

## Diffusion Model Data Synthesis & Anti-Leakage Workflow

- **Anti-Leakage Strategy**: Pratyaksh presented diffusion model results for generating synthetic background patches (128x128 / 256x256). To prevent data leakage, the team agreed to train the diffusion model on a clean 75% background split (excluding object-level training samples), generate background patches, paste external/procedurally generated objects, and test the classifier on the held-out 25%.
- **Latent Space Inpainting Trick**: Hemanth proposed a workflow to blend objects into generated backgrounds:
  1. Generate a background patch.
  2. Copy-paste an object onto the background.
  3. Encode the composite image to latent space via autoencoder.
  4. Add minor noise to the latent space.
  5. Run 1–2 d-noising diffusion steps to bring the object into the natural image distribution.

## Image-Domain Augmentations vs. XTF Operations

- **Shift to Image Domain**: Ratul presented XTF raw amplitude manipulation experiments (median background subtraction, height scaling x1.3, amplitude mixing), encountering gradient mismatch and scaling discrepancies between datasets.
- **Dataset Scaling Note**: Transferring objects from VW to ANTX caused scaling issues because ANTX samples have different metrics/resolutions.
  - *Note*: Gemini transcript mislabeled ANTX as "ENTx".
- **Image-Domain Augmentations**: The team (Pratyaksh & Hemanth) decided to adopt runtime image-domain augmentations (OpenCV Poisson copy-paste, rotation/scaling matrices, shadow augmentations) rather than static XTF manipulation.
- **Pixel Blending Rule**: When merging overlapping dark/light areas, calculate the mean of pixel values rather than summing to prevent clipping and overflow (>255).
