# Iris Sync - August 10, 2026

**Summary**: The team evaluated 5-fold cross-validation metrics across ~20 models, identifying Version 4 with image augmentation as the highest-recall configuration and designating V4 AUG + Cut-and-Paste as the leading model candidate for Milestone 2 pending final cross-validation testing. The team also conducted false positive analysis to mine missing ground truth annotations, established a four-table reporting structure (unprocessed vs. filtered), refined procedural shape generation using a radial boundary distance function, and proposed derivative-free evolutionary strategies (CMA-ES/CEM) to automate synthetic parameter optimization as the project transitions to shipping mode.

## Key Highlights & Discussion Points

### Model Selection & Milestone 2 Status
- **Cross-Validation Results & V4 Performance**: Cross-validation results across ~20 models/folds confirmed that Version 4 with image augmentation achieved the highest recall across folds. While precision was lower, it is addressable via post-processing filters (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Leading Candidate (V4 AUG + Cut-and-Paste)**: The team designated V4 AUG combined with cut-and-paste (copy-paste) augmentation as the leading candidate configuration for Milestone 2. Cross-validation testing is underway to validate performance gains before finalizing the model selection (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Model Lineage Clarification**: Clarified model configurations: V3 contains copy-paste augmentation, V4 utilizes image augmentation filters without copy-paste, and the new testing candidate combines V4 image augmentations with cut-and-paste targets (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Transition to Shipping Mode**: Hemanth advised transitioning from research mode to shipping mode to package Milestone 2 deliverables, with Geoff targeting external model delivery and Jetson onboard testing completion within the week (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### False Positive Analysis & Data Quality Mining
- **Unlabeled Contact Mining**: Visual inspection of false positives from the high-recall V4 model revealed that many flagged detections visually resemble genuine UXOs that lacked ground truth annotations or were omitted from the `AOI small black` class (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Ground Truth Error Rate**: Estimated labeling error in base ground truth at ~1–5%. The team resolved to use high-recall model predictions to execute one final, rapid re-labeling and cleaning pass to fix missing annotations, expecting precision to rise in tandem with ground truth corrections (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **UXO vs. AOI Small Black Capture**: Sachin confirmed manual mask capture across all files for confirmed UXOs, noting that any remaining missing annotations are likely within the `AOI small black` category (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Post-Processing & Four-Table Reporting Framework
- **Precision Improvement via Filtering**: To verify that post-processing can elevate precision without degrading recall, Sachin was tasked with sharing four performance tables on Slack:
  1. Unprocessed results across folds/models.
  2. Filtered/post-processed results (e.g. size thresholding, confidence filtering) demonstrating precision gains (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).

### Synthetic Data Procedural Generation & Evolutionary Optimization
- **Procedural Pipeline Architecture**: Real backgrounds with artificial procedurally generated foreground objects are prioritized first for initial benchmark metrics, followed by layered integration on diffusion-generated backgrounds (with roll, pitch, and sand ripple textures) (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Radial Boundary Distance Function**: Ratul developed a procedural shape generation approach based on calculating the distance from the center of a real object mask to its outer boundary as a function of elongation and height, generating novel realistic contours beyond standard geometric primitives (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Hyperparameter Boundaries**: Discarded rigid 0.1 drop percentages; established testing ranges between 0.3 and 0.9, converging on random uniform drop rates of 0.7–0.9 for background processing along with blend mask multiplier tuning and blur kernel adjustments (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **Evolutionary Optimization (CMA-ES / CEM)**: Hemanth proposed replacing manual visual parameter tuning with derivative-free evolutionary search algorithms (such as Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy [CMA-ES] or Cross-Entropy Method [CEM] from `scipy.optimize`) paired with structural and color-based loss functions to search the parameter and primitive space automatically (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
- **24-Hour Viability Deadline**: Established a 24-hour deadline to validate procedural synthetic sample generation and set up the training pipeline, with Pratyaksh executing 1–2 model iterations by Wednesday (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_10 12_27 EDT - Notes by Gemini.md).
