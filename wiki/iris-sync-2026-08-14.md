**Summary**: Details the August 14, 2026 team sync covering K-fold training throughput optimization (repeat factor reduction to 1, Blackwell MIG parallelization), 3-channel pre-trained synthetic experiments with Poisson copy-paste and procedural mines, clarification of previously reported 80/20 F1 metrics, data-driven shadow/highlight mask modulation, Treasure Island rocky background inference analysis, and Jetson onboard deployment preparation.

## Key Discussion Points & Insights

### 1. K-Fold Training Throughput Optimization & Compute Infrastructure
- **Diagnosis of 17+ Hour Folds**: Initial training runs for K-fold Fold 1 (Base V4, V4 heavy augmentation, and V4 AUG + Poisson copy-paste) took 17+ hours on single GPUs. The team identified two compounding factors:
  1. **Early Stopping Parameter**: Early stopping had been relaxed from 25 epochs up to 60–76 epochs without convergence stopping.
  2. **Dataloader Repeat Factor**: The `repeat_factor` parameter was set to 50 (originally written for small whole-image datasets to sample random crops 50 times per epoch), causing small pre-sliced K-fold tiles to be sampled 50 times per epoch—effectively running 3,000 passes per fold (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Pipeline Parameter Adjustments**:
  - Reduced `repeat_factor` from 50 to **1** (or 2 max) since chopped K-fold tiles are already pre-cropped.
  - Set total training epochs to **200**, establishing an early evaluation window at the first 10–30 epochs to identify the best-performing models.
  - Reduced OpenCV/NumPy multiprocessing worker processes from 8 to 4 and set thread limits to prevent CPU core contention and swap memory thrashing (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
  - Target training duration reduced to **3–4 hours per fold**.
- **Hardware Parallelization**:
  - Moved **V1** baseline training to the **Ninja** machine.
  - Assigned heavy **V4** configurations to **Volley** and **Blackwell** GPUs.
  - Configured the Blackwell platform with Multi-Instance GPU (MIG) support to train all 5 folds in parallel per model, targeting full cross-validation results within 24–48 hours (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).

### 2. Synthetic Data Augmentation & 3-Channel Pre-Training
- **3-Channel Grayscale Replication**: [[sss-augmentation-methods]] transitioned from single-channel grayscale input to a 3-channel setup (copying grayscale across RGB channels) initialized with pre-trained ImageNet weights. A differential learning rate was applied, setting the encoder learning rate to 0.1× of the decoder learning rate to preserve pre-trained feature extractors (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Poisson Copy-Paste + Procedural Mine Performance**:
  - Combining OpenCV Poisson copy-paste with procedural mine target augmentation achieved the highest recall on UXO classes, missing only 1 to 2 objects across validation sets.
  - Object-level F1 score at 0.1 IoU was ~67% due to aggressive high-recall detections (generating small false positives that can be filtered via post-processing size and confidence thresholds).
  - Confirmed that BCE + Dice loss outperformed Tversky loss in this pipeline configuration (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Data-Driven Mask Modulation (Ratul)**:
  - Shifted from rigid geometric primitives (circles, ovals, teardrops) to data-driven object masks based on pixel standard deviation thresholds (increased SD threshold from 0.5 to 1.5 pixels).
  - Enables independent elongation and bulging of bright acoustic highlights and dark acoustic shadows along specific axes to simulate varying sonar illumination angles.
  - Code committed to a dedicated branch on the Bedrock GitHub repository with entry point documentation (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).

### 3. Customer Metrics & Reporting Strategy
- **Correction on 90%+ F1 Score**: Sachin clarified that the previously reported 90%+ F1 score on the 80/20 split was artificially inflated because the evaluation script inadvertently merged `AOI big` and `black patch` into a single class alongside `UXO` + `AOI small black` (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **4-Bucket Confusion Matrix Standard**: The team reaffirmed that single F1 metrics can mislead client expectations. The primary deliverable for Bedrock will be **4-bucket confusion matrices** (presenting both raw object counts and percentages: UXO-like [UXO + AOI small black], Non-UXO-like [AOI Big, etc.], and Background) to demonstrate high recall on true targets while framing false positives clearly (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Test-Time Scaling / Multi-Model Inference**: Evaluated running multiple model variations or destructive augmentations (e.g. image inversion) in series at test time to boost precision without requiring parallel GPU compute. Agreed to explore this as an optional "test-time scaling" feature for Bedrock if experimental signals remain positive (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).

### 4. Treasure Island Dataset Evaluation & Domain Shift
- **V4 Model Performance**: Evaluated base V4 (M3 model) on the Treasure Island dataset. The model successfully detects visible UXO contacts (recovering verified multi-pass contacts), but exhibits false positive detections on rocky seabed terrain (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Rocky Terrain Mimicry**: Treasure Island features high-resolution rocky seabed topography underrepresented in current training splits. The acoustic shadows, curves, and highlight bumps of natural rocks visually resemble UXO features.
- **Ground Truth Structure**: Bedrock provided two annotation tiers: 347 verified contact sightings across multiple overlapping passes (derived from 61 original unique physical contacts) and unverified single-pass contacts.
- **Recommendations**: Incorporate rocky background tiles from Treasure Island into future model training sets, and leverage magnetometer fusion (e.g. Bedrock's specialized mag analysis software) to disambiguate non-ferrous rock outcrops from ferromagnetic ordnance (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).

Scheduled for weekend deployment testing ahead of the end-of-August Milestone 2 delivery hard stop (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).

## Decisions

- **Repeat Factor Reduction**: Reduced dataloader repeat factor to 1 and set total training epochs to 200 with an early evaluation window at 10–30 epochs to accelerate K-fold training (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Compute Allocation**: Shifted V1 baseline training to Ninja, heavy V4 models to Volley, and utilized Blackwell with MIG support to parallelize 5-fold cross-validation runs (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Customer Reporting Standard**: Standardized on 4-bucket confusion matrices (raw counts and percentages) as the primary customer deliverable for Milestone 2 (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Test-Time Scaling Strategy**: Explore test-time augmentations and sequential multi-model inference as an optional performance feature for client demonstration (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
- **Treasure Island Integration**: Update future training strategies to incorporate Treasure Island rocky background tiles to address seabed terrain domain gaps (source: Iris Sync - 2026_08_14).
